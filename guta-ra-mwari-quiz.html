About this project

A place to learn and reflect.

Study Guta RaMwari materials through short, source-linked quizzes.

Our purpose

Make the books easier to study, one topic at a time.

This learning space brings together the GRM Handbook, the Shona Combined Volume, and the Ndebele Hymn Book. Questions point back to a section, page, or hymn so learners can revisit the original context.

01

Learn from the sources

The platform keeps the book titles and original-language teaching titles visible alongside English study questions.

02

Practice with context

After each answer, learners see a short explanation and a reference to the source material.

03

Build at your own pace

Quiz progress is saved in this browser so learners can return to their study on this device.

The Combined Volume contents index lists 670 numbered teaching titles. The current quiz set is a starter selection and will grow as more chapters are reviewed.
